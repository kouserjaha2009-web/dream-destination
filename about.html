Welcome to Dream Destination

Dream Destination is a travel website project designed to help visitors discover exciting places, explore travel packages and plan their dream holidays from one convenient website.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make exploring destinations simple, inspiring and accessible. We bring destination information, sample packages and a trip-planning form together in one place.

What You Can Explore

Discover beaches, mountains, historic cities and cultural experiences. Compare illustrative package prices in Indian rupees and use our booking form to prepare a sample travel plan.

Why This Website?

This student project demonstrates website navigation, responsive styling, destination pages, package listings and interactive form handling using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
